# The pen's buttons pick the pointer and the eraser; selections rotate, group and have a menu

- The secondary pen button switches to the pointer and leaves it there; holding the primary erases until release. "Swap side buttons" swaps them, and the settings hint says so
- The pen menu holds Pen and Hand; the toolbar's select button is the pointer, with Lasso, Rectangle and "Snap angles to 15°"
- A selection has one rotate handle; turning shows "+15°"-style readouts and scaling "1.25x"-style ones beside the handle, in the app's pill colours, light and dark
- Rotation snaps to 15° steps with snapping on, and each move, scale or turn is one undo step
- Text boxes and images can be turned; bounds, hit tests, the lasso, the canvas, thumbnails and PNG, PDF and SVG exports follow, and the text editor turns with its box
- Holding still on a selection opens a menu: bring forward, send backward, group or ungroup, duplicate, delete
- Elements can be grouped; a group selects, transforms, duplicates, deletes and restacks as one and stays contiguous in the stacking order
- `note.json` gains optional `rotation` on text and images and `group` on every element; format stays 1 and unrotated, ungrouped notes are written byte for byte as before
- Rationale: see ADRs [2026-11-24-2149-pen-buttons-pointer-and-eraser](../adr/2026-11-24-2149-pen-buttons-pointer-and-eraser.md) and [2026-11-24-2149-rotation-and-groups-in-note-json](../adr/2026-11-24-2149-rotation-and-groups-in-note-json.md)
