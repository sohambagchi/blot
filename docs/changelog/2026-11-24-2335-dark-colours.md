# Dark mode keeps colours distinct, and the colour panel shows drawn colours

- In dark mode, darker colours now draw darker than lighter ones. Chromatic ink maps its lightness into the band 0.48–0.93 instead of being raised to one floor, so navy and mid-blue no longer draw alike and every palette chip draws differently ([ADR](../adr/2026-11-24-2329-dark-mode-ink-lightness-band.md))
- The quick black, red, blue and green draw as before. The darkest chromatic colours still read on the dark page at 3:1 contrast or more.
- In dark mode the colour panel's wheel, tone strip and palette chips show the colours as they draw. The hex field keeps the stored colour, with "draws as #XXXXXX" under it.
- The sample library's thumbnails use the same transform as the canvas
- Render tests draw a stroke for every palette chip in light and dark (`palette-*.png`), and new screenshots show the colour panel in both themes on a tablet and on a landscape phone
