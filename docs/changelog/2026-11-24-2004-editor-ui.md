# Editor chrome and library UI from the design

- `app.screenscratch.ui` holds the Compose UI from the Claude Design canvas, built on `compose.ui` and `compose.foundation` only. Colours, type, radii and CSS-style shadows are tokens in `ui.theme`; every icon is the design's SVG path data turned into an `ImageVector`, with dashed paths split into dashes.
- Inter is bundled as a 163 KB Latin subset ([ADR](../adr/2026-11-24-2004-bundle-inter-subset.md)).
- `ui.editor`: `EditorChrome` draws over a canvas slot. It has the back pill with the title, the undo, redo and export pill, page and zoom chips, and the floating toolbar with the pen, eraser, select, shape and line menus, text, image, quick colours and the colour panel. `Editor` switches to the phone viewer below 600 dp. Everything is driven by a plain `EditorUiState` and an `EditorActions` interface. `InMemoryEditor` implements the actions over in-memory state, for the preview and as a base for the real wiring.
- The toolbar is a top pill in landscape and a left rail in portrait. Dragging the handle moves it. A drop within 48 dp of an edge docks it there, as a pill on the top or bottom and a rail on the sides; anywhere else it floats. Menus and the colour panel open away from the docked edge. Tapping the handle collapses the toolbar to handle, pen, eraser and select, with undo only and the ink colour shown on the pen. Only the handle expands it.
- Auto-collapse: the canvas calls `InMemoryEditor.onWritingActivity(eventTime)` on pen down and on every move. After 15 s of writing, with no pause over 3 s, the toolbar collapses once. Expanding it by hand starts the count again.
- The colour panel has an HSV wheel drawn with sweep and radial gradients, and a tone strip from black through the colour to white. It also has a hex field (`#RGB` or `#RRGGBB`), four width presets, the ten monochrome palettes, and the "Pressure" and "Pen only" toggles.
- The export dialog matches `Main-export`: format, area, scale and background segments, light-colours and dot-grid switches, a preview slot, the output size, "Save to folder" and "Share…". The layers button and panel are not built.
- `ui.library`: `Library` shows the folder column, search, a notes grid with thumbnails (a placeholder when there is none), page chips, the card menu, the new-note button with four page kinds, and empty and no-match states. While `StorageState` is `NotChosen` or `PermissionLost`, a prompt to pick or re-grant the folder replaces it. Phones get a single-column layout with folder chips. `LibraryUiState` and `LibraryActions` drive it, and `FakeLibrary` serves the design's sample notes.
- `ui.settings`: the settings dialog has the storage folder (change or forget), theme (Light, Dark, System), the side-button swap, and About with the licences. "Pen only" stays in the colour panel. The design's auto-collapse delay setting is left out.
- "UI preview" on the main screen opens both screens with fake data. Its dot-grid stand-in canvas draws plain strokes, so auto-collapse can be tried with the pen.
- Dependencies added: `compose.ui.test.junit4` for unit tests only.
- Tests: Robolectric renders 31 states to `app/build/ui-shots/`: the editor in light and dark, landscape and portrait, expanded and collapsed, with panels, menus and the export dialog open, plus the library and phone layouts. Each was compared against headless-Chrome renders of the design artboards. Behaviour tests cover snapping, collapse and expand, auto-collapse timing, wheel hue and saturation, tone, hex parsing, menus, search, the card menu and the storage prompts.
