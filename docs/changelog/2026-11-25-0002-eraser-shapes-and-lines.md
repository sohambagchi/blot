# The eraser erases shapes and lines

- Both eraser modes remove a shape or line they touch, whole: a shape at its outline (anywhere inside when filled), a line along its path, elbows and curves included
- Strokes still erase whole or in part by mode; text boxes and images are never erased
- Rationale: see ADR [2026-11-25-0002-eraser-takes-shapes-and-lines-whole](../adr/2026-11-25-0002-eraser-takes-shapes-and-lines-whole.md)
