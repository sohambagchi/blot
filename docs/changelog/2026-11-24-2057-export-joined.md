# Export works from the editor and the library

- The editor's export dialog saves to a folder you pick or opens the share sheet, as PNG, PDF or SVG, for the whole note, the visible area or the selection
- The library card menu's export and share do the same for the whole note
- Text boxes are laid out by one function on screen, in thumbnails and in every export format, so they match
- The toolbar's shape, line, text, eraser and select choices drive the canvas tools; shape kinds the note can't hold yet draw rectangles, and elbow and curved lines draw straight ones, each with a message
- All 25 device tests pass on the Android 12 and Android 15 tablet emulators; the text-box test now types into the field directly, since injected keys arrive out of order on Android 12 while the keyboard connects
