# The toolbar puts colours in the middle, and each compact part expands with its own arrow

- Toolbar order: pen, eraser and pointer; then the four quick colours and the colour-panel chevron; then shapes, lines, text and image; on the portrait rail the colour panel opens beside the rail's middle
- Compact toolbar: an expand arrow replaces the drag handle, so it can't be dragged while compact; the handle shows only when expanded, where a tap collapses and a drag moves it
- Corner pills: when compact, the undo pill shows an arrow that restores Redo, Export and the title; expanding the toolbar no longer expands them
- Collapsing (the handle, or 15 s of writing) still collapses the toolbar and both pills together
- "Screen page" is called "Screen" on the page chip and library cards
