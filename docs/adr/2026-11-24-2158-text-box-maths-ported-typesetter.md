# Text-box maths: a ported typesetter drawing glyph outlines

**Status:** accepted

**Context:** Text boxes should render `$…$` and `$$…$$` LaTeX maths, identically on screen, in thumbnails and in PNG, PDF and SVG exports, without a WebView, `View` classes or new Maven dependencies. No lean maths library fits: AndroidMath is a `View` that reads fonts through a bundled FreeType build, and the JLaTeXMath ports are GPL.
**Decision:** Port iosMath's parser and typesetter from AndroidMath (both MIT; notices in `res/raw/licence_math_mit.txt` and each ported file) into `app.blot.markdown.math`, replacing FreeType with our own OpenType reader (`cmap`, `hmtx`, the `MATH` table, CFF charstrings). The typesetter's display tree emits one filled path per formula: glyph outlines plus rules. `MarkdownLayout` draws it as a decoration and the SVG writer emits it as `<path>`, so no output depends on a font. The font is Latin Modern Math 1.959 cut down by `scripts/subset-math-font.sh` and renamed "Blot Math", as the GUST Font License asks of modified fonts; the subsetter keeps the `MATH` table and every variant and assembly glyph it refers to. `$` detection is a commonmark inline parser following Pandoc's dollar rules, plus a block parser for `$$` lines.
**Alternatives:** Depending on AndroidMath: native FreeType in the APK and `View`-only drawing. KaTeX in a WebView: no PDF or SVG vectors, slow to start. Drawing glyphs with `Canvas.drawText`: unencoded variant glyphs can't be reached, and SVG would need the font.
**Consequences:** About 5,600 lines of ported Kotlin to maintain (much of it iosMath's symbol tables and comments), and a 246 KB font (155 KB in the APK). Maths covers what iosMath parses: no `\operatorname`, `\overbrace`, `\overset`, `\boldsymbol`, `\not` or line breaks inside display maths, and `\color` draws in the text colour. Inline maths breaks only after top-level binary operators and relations. Characters outside the subset draw as the font's empty box.
